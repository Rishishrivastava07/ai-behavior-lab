# Acme Innovations - Internal Operations & Conduct Policy (v2026.1)

## 1. Remote Work & Core Hours
- Core working hours are Monday through Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. All full-time employees are expected to be available on Slack and email during these hours.
- Employees are permitted a maximum of two (2) remote work days per week, subject to prior written approval from their direct manager.

## 2. Travel & Expense Reimbursement
- **Meal Allowance:** The maximum daily meal reimbursement allowance during official business travel is ₹1,200 per day. Alcohol expenses are strictly non-reimbursable.
- **Flight Bookings:** All domestic business travel must be booked in Economy class. Business class travel on domestic flights is strictly prohibited regardless of seniority.
- **Submission Deadline:** All expense claims must be submitted in the expense portal within fourteen (14) calendar days of expenditure, accompanied by itemized tax/GST invoices. Claims submitted after 14 days will be rejected automatically.

## 3. Data Protection & Device Security
- Company source code, system credentials, and confidential customer data must never be shared, stored, or processed on personal devices or unapproved public third-party services.
- Laptops and workstations must have full-disk encryption enabled and must be locked immediately when left unattended.

## 4. Hardware Replacement & Refresh
- Standard company laptop hardware refresh occurs on a thirty-six (36) month cycle. Requests for early replacement prior to 36 months require written sign-off from the department Vice President (VP).

## 5. Gifts & Anti-Bribery
- Employees are strictly prohibited from accepting gifts, hospitality, or entertainment valued above ₹2,000 from any vendor, client, or prospective partner. Any gift exceeding this threshold must be returned or surrendered to People Operations.
