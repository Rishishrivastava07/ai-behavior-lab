Engineering Takeaway: The Limits of In-Context Steering

Notice that even with strict system prompting, the 1.5B model still hallucinated on maternity leave and stock ticker. Its pre-trained parametric weights overpowered the system instructions. This benchmark provides the exact empirical baseline we need to measure the success of Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT / LoRA) in future milestones.

Acme Innovations - Internal Operations & Conduct Policy

Version 2026.1 | Grounding Source for AI Behavior Lab

policy.md

1. Remote Work & Core Hours

Core working hours are Monday through Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. Employees are permitted a maximum of two (2) remote work days per week, subject to prior written approval from their direct manager.

2. Travel & Expense Reimbursement

Meal Allowance: The maximum daily meal reimbursement allowance during official business travel is ₹1,500 per day. Alcohol expenses are strictly non-reimbursable.
Flight Bookings: All domestic business travel must be booked in Economy class. Business class travel on domestic flights is strictly prohibited regardless of seniority.
Submission Deadline: All expense claims must be submitted within fourteen (14) calendar days of expenditure with itemized tax/GST invoices.

3. Data Protection & Device Security

Company source code, system credentials, and confidential customer data must never be stored on personal devices or unapproved public third-party services. Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must be locked immediately when unattended.

4. Hardware Replacement & Refresh

Standard company laptop refresh occurs on a thirty-six (36) month cycle. Requests for early replacement require written sign-off from the department Vice President (VP).

5. Gifts & Anti-Bribery

Employees are strictly prohibited from accepting gifts, hospitality, or entertainment valued above ₹2,000 from any vendor, client, or prospective partner. Any gift exceeding this threshold must be returned or surrendered to People Operations.
